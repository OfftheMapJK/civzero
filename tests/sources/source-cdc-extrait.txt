Extrait de « How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency », CDC (domaine public), pour les tests de l'agent.
If the water is cloudy, first filter it through a clean cloth, paper towel, or coffee filter, or allow it to settle.
Use unscented household chlorine bleach. Bleach at 5% to 9% sodium hypochlorite: 1 quart or liter, add 2 drops of bleach or 0.1 mL.
1 gallon: add 8 drops of bleach, ½ mL. Bleach at 1%: 1 quart or liter, add 10 drops, ½ mL.
If the water is cloudy, murky, colored, or very cold, add double the amount of bleach.
Stir and let stand for at least 30 minutes before you drink it.
Store the disinfected water in clean, sanitized containers with tight covers.
