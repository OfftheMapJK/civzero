# Charge your phone from the batteries at home

Many homes already have an energy reserve without knowing it: the battery of a laptop or an e-bike holds several times that of a phone.

## 1. Principle

Find the battery devices that are already charged and use them as a power bank, through their USB socket or through an adapter designed for it. Never connect a phone directly to a bare battery: only to a USB output provided by the manufacturer.

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: GREEN (circle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Tool battery variant: orange.
>
> Risks specific to this skill:
> - Tool battery variant: only use a USB adapter designed for that brand of battery. Never make your own connection on the terminals of a tool battery: a short circuit of a lithium battery can cause a fire.
> - A swollen, hot, damaged or burnt-smelling battery is no longer used: keep it away from anything that burns (*dead battery* skill).
> - Do not open devices to reach their battery.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Take stock

- **Action:** list the battery devices at home and their charge level: laptop, tablet, UPS, e-bike or e-scooter, tool batteries, portable speaker with a USB output.
- **Check:** you know which ones are charged and which ones have a USB socket.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 2: The laptop

- **Action:** plug the phone into a USB port of the laptop while it is on. Some laptops also power their USB ports when asleep or off, if the option is turned on in their settings. Close the screen or turn it down to the minimum so it uses less. [TO CHECK: name of the option depending on the manufacturer]
- **Check:** the phone shows that it is charging.
- **Source:** laptop manual

### Step 3: The UPS

- **Action:** if a UPS protects a computer or an Internet box, plug the phone's charger into it, and nothing else. The UPS drains fast: keep it for the priority phone. [TO CHECK: precautions depending on the model]
- **Check:** the phone is charging, the UPS does not signal an overload.
- **Source:** UPS manual

### Step 4: The e-bike or e-scooter

- **Action:** some e-bike batteries or displays have a USB socket. Check in the manual that it can charge a phone. Without a USB socket designed for it, do not improvise anything.
- **Check:** the USB socket exists and the phone is charging.
- **Source:** bike manual

### Step 5: Tool batteries (orange variant)

- **Action:** off-the-shelf USB adapters fit onto power tool batteries. You need the adapter of the same brand and range as the battery.
- **Check:** the adapter is the one designed for the battery; the phone charges without anything heating up.
- **Source:** adapter manual

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 2 — the laptop does not charge when it is off.** Switch it on and turn the screen down to the minimum, or look for the USB charging option in its settings once the power is back.
- **Step 3 — the UPS keeps beeping.** It is the signal that it is running on battery. Unplug everything that is not the phone charger.
- **Step 4 — no USB socket on the bike.** Move on to another source: do not connect anything directly to the bike's battery.
