# Keep a phone charged during an outage

The phone receives alerts, serves as a torch, a map and a library. Making it last is the first thing to do when the power goes out.

## 1. Principle

Two levers: use less, and recharge from a reserve. A phone searching for a missing network drains fast; the screen is often the biggest consumer.

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: GREEN (circle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Risks specific to this skill:
> - A swollen, hot or damaged power bank: stop using it (see the *dead battery* skill).

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Reduce consumption

- **Action:** turn on power-saving mode, lower the brightness, switch off Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location when not in use. On some models, the strongest saving mode also disables features such as hotspot sharing: check what it turns off on yours.
- **Check:** the power-saving indicator is on.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 2: Switch to airplane mode when there is no network

- **Action:** if the phone shows “no service”, turn on airplane mode and only turn it off at set times to check messages (see the *Communication plan* skill).
- **Check:** the airplane icon is shown outside contact times.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 3: Recharge from a power bank

- **Action:** plug the phone in. To estimate the number of charges: power bank energy in Wh × efficiency ÷ phone battery energy. Count on an efficiency of about two thirds, conversion and charging losses included (order of magnitude).
- **Check:** the phone shows that it is charging.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 4: Choose which phone to charge first

- **Action:** give priority to the phone that receives alerts and holds the communication plan.
- **Check:** that phone is above 50%.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 5: Keep a source of information without the phone

- **Action:** have a battery or crank radio (*Radio listening* skill).
- **Check:** the radio works without the phone.
- **Source:** not applicable

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 3 — the power bank does not charge the phone.** Try another cable or another port. Some cables only carry data or handle current poorly.
- **Step 1 — the phone still drains.** Close open apps and switch it off completely between contact times.
