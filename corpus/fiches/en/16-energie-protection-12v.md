# Measuring and protecting a 12 V circuit — fuse and wire size

Every skill that connects a device to a battery goes through this one. **No figure in this skill should be applied before review.**

## 1. Principle

A 12 V battery delivers a low voltage but can supply a very high current in a short circuit: the wire heats up until its insulation melts and catches fire. The fuse protects **the wire**, not the device. It goes as close as possible to the positive terminal, so that all the wire downstream is protected. The wire size depends on the current and the length; at 12 V, a wire that is too thin and too long also loses a lot of voltage.

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 6 — continuity between + and −.** There is a short circuit: do not insert the fuse, check everything again.
- **Step 7 — the fuse blows immediately.** Short circuit or rating too low: remove it, check step 6 again before changing anything.
- **Step 8 — a wire heats up.** Switch off; the wire size is too small or a connection is loose.
