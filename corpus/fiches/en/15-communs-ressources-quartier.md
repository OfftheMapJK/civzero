# Map the resources of your neighbourhood

Know what exists within walking distance: water points, public places, workshops, skills. It is the first inventory of an interstice.

## 1. Principle

During an outage, what is nearby becomes the horizon again. Towns already publish part of the information; in France, the DICRIM (the town's information document on major risks) and the municipal emergency plan. The rest is mapped on foot and built with the neighbours.

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: GREEN (circle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Risks specific to this skill: personal data. List skills and places, never a person without their explicit agreement, and never a private address.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Read what the town publishes

- **Action:** look up your town's risk information document (the DICRIM in France), and ask the town hall for the municipal emergency plan and the shelters planned in a crisis.
- **Check:** you know the listed risks and the planned shelters.
- **Source:** the town's risk document

### Step 2: Set the area

- **Action:** draw on the map the area you can reach on foot in a reasonable time.
- **Check:** the area is drawn on the paper map.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 3: Map the useful places

- **Action:** walk the area and note: public water points, pharmacies, town hall, shelters, local shops. Only shared places or places open to the public.
- **Check:** each place is on the map.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 4: Map the places of know-how

- **Action:** workshops, fablabs, repair cafés, shared gardens, associations.
- **Check:** each place is on the map with its type.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 5: List skills, with agreement

- **Action:** in the communication plan group, note who knows how to do what (repair a bike, give care, fix things), only with each person's agreement and without addresses.
- **Check:** each skill noted has the person's agreement.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 6: Transfer and share

- **Action:** transfer the places to the offline map and the paper map, and share the map with the group.
- **Check:** each member of the group has the map.
- **Source:** *Offline map of your region* skill

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 1 — the town has no risk document online.** Ask at the town hall; it is often available on paper.
- **Step 5 — some people refuse to be listed.** That is their right: only note the places.
- **Step 3 — the neighbourhood is large.** Share the mapping between the members of the group.
