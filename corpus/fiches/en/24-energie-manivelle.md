# Charge your phone with a hand crank

A crank works at night, in the rain and with nothing else. In return, it takes a lot of effort for little energy.

## 1. Principle

Turning the crank drives a small dynamo that charges the device's internal battery, which can then charge a phone through its USB output. The energy you get is small: count in minutes of calls, not in battery percentage. It is an emergency reserve, not a regular source. [TO CHECK: order of magnitude of energy per minute of cranking]

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: GREEN (circle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Risks specific to this skill: do not force the crank, which breaks easily; turn steadily rather than fast.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Choose a device that can charge

- **Action:** check on the device or its manual that it has a USB output able to charge a phone, not just an input to charge itself.
- **Check:** the USB output is marked.
- **Source:** device manual

### Step 2: Crank before plugging in

- **Action:** turn the crank steadily to charge the internal battery first, at the pace given in the manual.
- **Check:** the device's charging light comes on.
- **Source:** device manual

### Step 3: Plug in the phone switched off or in airplane mode

- **Action:** plug in the phone, in airplane mode or switched off, so that all the energy goes to the battery.
- **Check:** the phone shows that it is charging.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 4: Aim for a specific use

- **Action:** charge for a use decided in advance: a call, reading messages at the contact time of the communication plan.
- **Check:** the planned use could be done.
- **Source:** not applicable

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 3 — the phone does not respond.** A completely empty phone may need a little charge before it switches on: leave it plugged in while you keep turning.
- **Step 2 — the light does not come on.** Turn in the direction shown on the device, and more steadily.
