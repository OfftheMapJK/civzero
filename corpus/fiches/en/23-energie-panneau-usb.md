# Charge your phone with a small USB solar panel

The simplest way to generate your own energy: a folding panel, a cable, some sun. It does not replace a socket, but it lets you hold out for days.

## 1. Principle

A small USB panel only supplies current when it receives direct sun. At the slightest cloud, its power drops and many phones stop and restart charging. So it is better to charge a **power bank** first, which accepts irregular charging, then charge the phone from it. The power shown on the packaging is only reached in full sun, with the panel facing the sun. [TO CHECK: usual gap between announced and real power]

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Read the panel

- **Action:** note on the label the voltage and current of the USB output (*Read a label* skill). An output of 5 V is expected.
- **Check:** you know the panel's output.
- **Source:** panel label

### Step 2: Point the panel

- **Action:** unfold the panel in full sun, with no shade, even partial, facing the sun. Turn it again as the sun moves.
- **Check:** the panel's indicator light, if it has one, is on.
- **Source:** panel manual

### Step 3: Charge the power bank

- **Action:** plug the power bank into the panel and put it in the shade.
- **Check:** the power bank's charging light comes on.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 4: Or charge the phone directly

- **Action:** without a power bank, plug in the phone, put it in the shade and switch it off or put it in airplane mode so it uses less while charging.
- **Check:** the phone charges, even intermittently.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 5: Put it away before night

- **Action:** fold the panel and bring the equipment in before night or rain, unless the panel is designed for outdoor use. [TO CHECK: water resistance depending on the model]
- **Check:** the equipment is stored in the dry.
- **Source:** panel manual

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 4 — the phone keeps stopping and restarting.** The sun is too weak or hazy: charge a power bank instead, or wait for clear sun.
- **Step 2 — nothing happens behind a window.** Glass greatly reduces what the panel receives: put it outside.
- **Step 3 — the power bank does not charge.** Try another cable; some power banks need more current than the panel supplies.
