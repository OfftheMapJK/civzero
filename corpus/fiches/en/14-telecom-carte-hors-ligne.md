# Offline map of your region

Have a map that works without a network, on a phone and on paper. It supports the skill *Map the resources of your neighbourhood*.

## 1. Principle

Free apps based on OpenStreetMap download the map of a whole region to the phone. Once downloaded, it works in airplane mode, and satellite positioning works without a mobile network. The paper map remains the ultimate backup.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Install an offline map app

- **Action:** install Organic Maps or OsmAnd, two apps based on OpenStreetMap.
- **Check:** the app opens.
- **Source:** the apps' websites (see sources)

### Step 2: Download your region

- **Action:** in the app, download the region or county, with the Internet.
- **Check:** the region appears as downloaded.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 3: Test in airplane mode

- **Action:** turn on airplane mode and move around the map, then ask for your position. Without a mobile network, the first fix can take several minutes, under open sky. [TO CHECK: does positioning stay active in airplane mode on all phones?]
- **Check:** the map appears and your position is shown.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 4: Add your landmarks

- **Action:** mark your home, the meeting points of the communication plan and the neighbourhood's resources.
- **Check:** the landmarks appear in airplane mode.
- **Source:** *Map the resources of your neighbourhood* skill

### Step 5: Print a paper map

- **Action:** print the neighbourhood and its surroundings, and add the landmarks by hand.
- **Check:** the paper map is stored with the 72-hour kit.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 6: Update

- **Action:** update the map regularly when you have the Internet.
- **Check:** the date of the downloaded map is recent.
- **Source:** not applicable

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 2 — not enough space on the phone.** Only download the county.
- **Step 3 — the position does not appear.** Go out into the open and wait a few minutes.
- **Step 5 — no printer.** Copy the main streets and your landmarks by hand.
