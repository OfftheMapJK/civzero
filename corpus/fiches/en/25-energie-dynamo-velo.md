# Charge your phone with a bike dynamo

Every bike ride can fill a battery. The power is low but steady, and depends neither on the sun nor on the grid.

## 1. Principle

A bike dynamo produces a low-power alternating current, designed for lighting: a phone cannot use it as it is. A **USB charger for dynamos** rectifies and regulates it; models with a **buffer battery** store the energy and charge the phone afterwards, even when stopped. You need to ride fast enough for the charger to start. [TO CHECK: power of common hub dynamos and the speed at which charging starts]

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: ORANGE (triangle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Risks specific to this skill:
> - A badly refitted wheel can come off while riding: serious fall. Have the tightening checked by someone who knows, or a bike workshop, if you have never removed a wheel.
> - Never handle the phone while riding. Keep it in a bag, not on the handlebars within reach.
> - Never connect a phone directly to the wires of a dynamo.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Identify your dynamo

- **Action:** find out whether the bike has a hub dynamo (a bigger front hub, with two wires) or a bottle dynamo (rubbing on the tyre). Read what is written on it (*Read a label* skill).
- **Check:** you know the type of dynamo and its voltage.
- **Source:** markings on the dynamo

### Step 2: Choose the charger

- **Action:** choose a USB charger designed for bike dynamos, compatible with the dynamo's voltage, preferably with a buffer battery.
- **Check:** the charger's manual states it is compatible with your dynamo.
- **Source:** charger manual

### Step 3: Connect and secure

- **Action:** connect the charger to the dynamo's wires according to its manual, secure the case and the cables so they touch neither the wheel nor the spokes.
- **Check:** nothing rubs when you spin the wheel by hand.
- **Source:** charger manual

### Step 4: Try it while riding

- **Action:** ride on a safe route, with no phone plugged in, and watch the charger's light.
- **Check:** the light shows that the buffer battery is charging.
- **Source:** charger manual

### Step 5: Charge the phone

- **Action:** plug the phone into the buffer battery, when stopped or in a bag during the ride.
- **Check:** the phone shows that it is charging.
- **Source:** not applicable

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 4 — the light does not come on.** Ride faster: charging only starts above a certain speed. Then check the connections with a multimeter (*Use a multimeter* skill).
- **Step 1 — the bike has no dynamo.** A front wheel with a hub dynamo fits most bikes; it is a job for a bike workshop. [TO CHECK: compatibility of mountings and brakes]
- **Step 5 — the phone charges then stops.** A charger without a buffer battery cuts out each time you slow down: charge a power bank first.
