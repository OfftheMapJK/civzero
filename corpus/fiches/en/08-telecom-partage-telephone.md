# Pass skills from phone to phone

Spread the CivicNode collection without the Internet or a station: a phone that has it can give it to another.

## 1. Principle

Phones can exchange files directly, without a mobile network: through their system's nearby sharing, through Bluetooth, or through a cable or a USB stick. The skills collection is small; offline Wikipedia is much larger and transfers poorly over wireless.

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: GREEN (circle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Risks specific to this skill: only accept files from someone you know.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Have the collection as a file

- **Action:** on the giving phone, find the archive of the CivicNode skills or the `.zim` file read by the Kiwix app.
- **Check:** you find the file in the file manager and know its size.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 2: Choose the transfer method

- **Action:** between two Android phones, nearby sharing (Quick Share); between two iPhones, AirDrop. Between Android and iPhone, direct exchange only exists on some recent phones (the Pixel 10 since November 2025). Otherwise, and for large files, a dual-connector USB stick (USB-C and USB-A), on a phone that accepts sticks. Bluetooth as a last resort.
- **Check:** the two phones see each other, or the stick is recognised.
- **Source:** TechCrunch, 20 November 2025 (see sources)

### Step 3: Send the small file first

- **Action:** send the skills archive before any large file.
- **Check:** the transfer finishes without error.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 4: Check on the receiving phone

- **Action:** open the archive in the browser or the file in Kiwix on the receiving phone.
- **Check:** the CivicNode home page or the Kiwix home page appears.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 5: Check the version date

- **Action:** compare the sync date shown on the two phones.
- **Check:** both have the same version, or you know which one is the most recent.
- **Source:** not applicable

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 2 — the two phones do not see each other.** Turn on Bluetooth and visibility on both, bring them closer, or use a USB stick.
- **Step 3 — the transfer stops.** File too large for Bluetooth: use the cable or the stick.
- **Step 4 — the archive does not open in the browser.** Some mobile browsers block local files. [TO CHECK: compatible browsers] Try the Kiwix app.
