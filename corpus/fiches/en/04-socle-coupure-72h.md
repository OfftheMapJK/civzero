# Prepare for a 72-hour outage — energy and communication

The entry point for preparing. This skill covers light, information, energy and communication. Water, food and health are covered by the official guide listed below.

## 1. Principle

In March 2025, the European Commission recommended that every household be able to cope on its own for at least 72 hours. In France, the SGDSN's “Tous responsables” guide, published in November 2025, describes an emergency kit of this kind. This skill takes up the energy and communication part and links it to the other CivicNode skills.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Read the official guide for water, food and health

- **Action:** download the official preparedness guide for your country (in France, “Tous responsables”, also available at the town hall) and prepare those parts separately.
- **Check:** you have the guide and a list for water, food and medication.
- **Source:** “Tous responsables” guide, SGDSN (see sources)

### Step 2: Light

- **Action:** one head torch or torch per person, and spare batteries of the right size.
- **Check:** every lamp lights up, and its spare batteries are stored with it.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

### Step 3: Information

- **Action:** a radio that works without mains power (batteries, crank or solar) and the list of local stations on paper.
- **Check:** you can pick up a local station with the mains unplugged.
- **Source:** *Radio listening* skill

### Step 4: Phone power

- **Action:** a charged power bank and its cable.
- **Check:** it charges the phone (*Keep a phone charged* skill).
- **Source:** *Keep a phone charged* skill

### Step 5: Communication

- **Action:** make the communication plan with your family and write the important numbers on paper.
- **Check:** every member of the household has a paper copy of the plan.
- **Source:** *Communication plan for family and neighbours* skill

### Step 6: Paper

- **Action:** gather in a folder: emergency numbers, communication plan, neighbourhood map, list of chargers and their devices.
- **Check:** the folder is stored with the rest of the kit.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 7: Check regularly

- **Action:** set a check date: batteries, power bank charge, radio working. [TO CHECK: recommended frequency]
- **Check:** the date of the next check is written on the kit.
- **Source:** [SOURCE NEEDED]

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 2 — limited budget.** One head torch per household and batteries are better than nothing. Add more over time.
- **Step 3 — no station comes through.** Move near a window, extend the aerial. See the *Radio listening* skill.
- **Step 7 — you forget to check.** Tie the check to a fixed date: the clock change, the start of the school year.

## 5. Resources

### Checklist (energy and communication)

- Lamps and spare batteries
- Stand-alone radio and list of stations
- Charged power bank and cable
- Communication plan on paper
- Paper folder: numbers, map, list of chargers
