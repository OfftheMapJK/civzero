# Make water safe by boiling it

When tap water is no longer safe, or all you have is water you found, boiling is the safest way to kill germs. It only takes a pot and a flame.

## 1. Principle

Heat kills bacteria, viruses and parasites. A full rolling boil for one minute is enough; at altitude, water boils at a lower temperature and you need to boil it longer. Cloudy water is cleared first. Boiling does not remove fuel, chemicals or radioactive material: water polluted that way is still to be avoided.

## 2. Safety

> **LEVEL: GREEN (circle)** — [Official label shown from Nodule Zero]
>
> Camping stove variant during an outage: orange.
>
> Risks specific to this skill:
> - Burns: boiling water and a hot pot. Let it cool before pouring, keep children away.
> - Stove variant: a camping gas stove, a barbecue or a brazier is never lit indoors, nor in a garage. Their fumes contain carbon monoxide, odourless and deadly.
> - Water containing fuel, chemicals or radioactive material does not become safe by boiling.

## 3. Step by step

### Step 1: Clear cloudy water

- **Action:** if the water is cloudy, filter it through a clean cloth, a paper towel or a coffee filter, or let it settle and draw off the clear water at the top.
- **Check:** the water poured into the pot is clear.
- **Source:** s1

### Step 2: Bring to a rolling boil

- **Action:** bring the water to a full rolling boil, then keep it boiling for 1 minute. Above 2,000 metres of altitude, keep it boiling for 3 minutes.
- **Check:** large bubbles keep rising from the bottom for the whole time.
- **Source:** s1 (1 minute; 3 minutes above 6,500 feet, about 2,000 metres) [TO CHECK: conversion]

### Step 3: Let it cool, covered

- **Action:** turn off the heat, cover the pot and let it cool without dipping anything into the water.
- **Check:** the pot feels lukewarm or cold to the touch.
- **Source:** not applicable

### Step 4: Store it cleanly

- **Action:** pour the water into clean, sanitised containers that close tightly.
- **Check:** each container is closed and dated.
- **Source:** s1

### Step 5: Improve the taste if needed

- **Action:** boiled water tastes flat. Pour it back and forth between containers and let it stand for a few hours, or add a pinch of salt per litre.
- **Check:** the taste is acceptable for drinking.
- **Source:** s1

## 4. If you get stuck

- **Step 2 — not enough fuel to boil all the water.** Keep boiling for drinking and cooking water, and disinfect washing water another way (*Disinfect water with chlorine* skill).
- **Step 1 — the water stays cloudy after filtering.** Let it settle longer, then filter again.
- **The water smells of fuel or chemicals.** Do not drink it, even boiled: look for another source and alert the town hall or health services.

## 5. Resources

### What the source says

The CDC presents boiling as the best method for killing germs, ahead of chemical disinfection, which works less well against some parasites such as *Cryptosporidium* and *Giardia* (source s1).
