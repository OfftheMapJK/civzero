## 1. Why a trademark

Everything CivicNode publishes is open: the skills are under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, the code under AGPL-3.0. Anyone may copy, modify and redistribute them.

The name and logo say something else: that the skills you are reading went through our verification chain (see [Our sources and our method](a-sources-et-methode.html)). If any modified version could carry that name, it would no longer guarantee anything. That is why the name “CivicNode” and the logo are not under CC BY-SA: they are the project's trademarks.

## 2. What you may do without asking

- Talk about the project, quote it, criticise it, write about it.
- Link to the site or the repository.
- Show the logo, unchanged, to refer to the project (article, poster, talk, list of resources).
- Build a “CivicNode station” and call it that, as long as it serves the official collection unmodified. You may add other content (Wikipedia, maps, local documents) if it is clearly separate from the skills.
- Credit CivicNode as the source of what you reuse: the licence of the skills requires it.

## 3. What you may do if you say so

You may modify the skills, translate them, adapt them to your region: the licence allows it. In that case:

- give your version another name, and do not use the logo as your own;
- say where it comes from, for example “based on CivicNode”;
- say that you changed it, and keep the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

A correction or a translation can also join the official collection: propose it, and it will go through the same verification chain as the other skills.

## 4. What is not allowed

- Distributing modified skills under the name “CivicNode” or with its logo.
- Suggesting a partnership, support or endorsement by the project that does not exist.
- Collecting money, donations or data in the project's name.
- Altering the logo, or using it as the logo of another project, product or organisation.

## 5. A doubt, a request

Write to hello@civicnode.eu. If your use fits none of the cases above, ask: the answer is usually yes.

## 6. Where the trademark stands

The logo is being designed. The name and logo are not yet registered: registration is planned, and this page will then say who holds them. Until then, this policy states how we ask that the name be used.
