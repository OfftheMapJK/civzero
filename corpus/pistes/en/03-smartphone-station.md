# An old smartphone as a pocket knowledge station

## 1. The idea

Many phones end up in a drawer with a cracked screen or a tired battery, but with a processor and memory that still work. The free Kiwix app reads Wikipedia and other collections offline, compressed in the ZIM format. A salvaged phone loaded with Wikipedia, the CivicNode skills and a regional map becomes a small knowledge station: it costs nothing, uses little power, and can be charged with a small solar panel (*Charge your phone with a small USB solar panel* skill). By sharing its Wi-Fi, it could serve this content to nearby phones. [TO CHECK: library-sharing feature of the Kiwix Android app]

## 2. Where it stands

The tools exist and are proven: Kiwix runs on Android and other systems, and is already used in schools without Internet, in prison education programmes and aboard ships. What remains to be tested is using an old phone as a neighbourhood station: how long it lasts, how many devices it can serve, how much power it uses, how its battery ages.

**Maturity: prototype.**

## 3. Who is working on it

- The Kiwix project, free software under the GPL licence, and its content collections.
- Repair cafés and reuse workshops, which recover and refurbish phones.
- CivicNode, which wants to test it as a lightweight station alongside the mini-PC.

## 4. Open questions

- The real battery life of an old phone serving several devices.
- The battery: an old battery can swell and become dangerous.
- Security: a phone without updates should only be used offline.
- Space: does the full French or English Wikipedia, with or without images, fit in an old model's memory?

## 5. How to contribute

- Load an old phone with Kiwix and a collection, then measure: battery life, devices served, power use.
- Send CivicNode a report with the phone model and the results.
- Suggest the trial at a repair café.

## 6. Caution

This lead describes an ongoing trial, not a proven method. A phone with a swollen, hot or warped battery is no longer used: see the *What to do with a dead battery or device* skill. Never open a phone to change its battery as part of this lead.
