Inclination 98.57° toward the Equator Meteor ← Meteor-M No.2-2 Meteor-M No.2-4 → The Meteor-M No.2-3
is a weather satellite , designed, built and operated by the Roscosmos , Russia's state-owned space
corporation. The satellite was launched in 2023, on board a Soyuz-2-1b launch vehicle , equipped
with a Fregat-M upper stage. [ 1 ] It is the 5th flight of the Meteor-M series, and the 6th of the
Meteor-3M program; the satellite has an expected service life of five years or more. [ 2 ] Like its
predecessors, Meteor-M No.2-3 was built to watch global weather, record characteristics of the Ozone
Layer , measure temperatures of the earth's oceans and track icing conditions in the polar regions
to facilitate shipping . [ 3 ] MSU-MR image received from Meteor-M No.2-3 by a hobbyist showing the
east coast of the United States. Relevance for hobbyists [ edit ] As with the other Meteor
satellites, Meteor-M No.2-3 transmits a continuous feed of both LRPT and HRPT imagery as well as
some sensor data, and most transmissions are unencrypted and free, and can be received by anyone
with basic, inexpensive equipment, such as an SDR and a basic dipole (also widely known as
Rabbit-Ears) TV antenna. [ 4 ] References [ edit ] ↑ "Meteor-M no. 2-3 / WAI Hub" .
